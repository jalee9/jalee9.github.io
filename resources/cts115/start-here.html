Welcome to CTS 115

Your guide to learning how technology supports business, organizations, and the work of IT professionals.

Start Here

Technology touches nearly every part of a modern organization. In this course, you'll explore how businesses use technology, how technology influences decisions and strategy, and why understanding the business side of IT matters—regardless of which IT career path you pursue.

This site is your learning guide for each module. Instead of long lectures, you'll work through short explanations, real-world examples, visuals, and opportunities to apply what you're learning.
